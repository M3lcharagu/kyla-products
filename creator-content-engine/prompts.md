## 20 prompts

1. **Video ideas** — Generate 20 short-form video ideas for [niche] aimed at [audience]. For each, include the viewer problem, format, one-sentence angle, proof or example I can gather, and a simple CTA. Do not invent results.
2. **Script builder** — Write a [length]-second vertical-video script about [topic] with a hook, three clear beats, spoken words, on-screen text, shot suggestions, and a natural CTA. Use placeholders for any fact I must verify.
3. **Hook lab** — Create 25 non-clickbait hooks for [topic] in five styles: question, mistake, myth, demonstration, and story. Keep each under [word count] words and avoid guaranteed outcomes.
4. **Caption pack** — Write 10 captions for this video, each with a distinct tone, one useful takeaway, a conversation question, and a CTA. Include platform-friendly line breaks and relevant placeholders: [video summary].
5. **Content calendar** — Build a [number]-day calendar for [niche] with three content pillars, a daily format, topic, hook, CTA, and repurposing note. Match my posting capacity of [hours or posts per week].
6. **Batch planner** — Turn these ideas into one filming session. Group by location, props, outfit, and energy level; give a shot list and a simple naming system: [ideas].
7. **Long-form to shorts** — Extract [number] short-video opportunities from this long-form transcript. For each, quote only the supplied text, give a hook, a self-contained edit, caption, and timestamp: [transcript].
8. **Repurposing matrix** — Convert this [podcast, webinar, or article] into posts for short video, carousel, email, and story. Preserve the original meaning and flag claims needing verification: [source].
9. **Fitness niche** — Create 15 responsible fitness video concepts for [audience and goal]. Include a safety note where relevant, a beginner-friendly variation, and a CTA to seek qualified guidance; make no medical promises.
10. **Podcast niche** — Plan 10 podcast episodes for [audience] with title, central question, three segments, guest profile, opening hook, and one short-form clip angle. Avoid repeating these topics: [list].
11. **Salon niche** — Create 12 salon content ideas covering services, preparation, aftercare, FAQs, behind the scenes, trust, and bookings. Use verified details and inclusive language; include WhatsApp and Instagram CTAs.
12. **Trading niche** — Create 12 educational trading content ideas covering process, journaling, risk, market vocabulary, and lessons from hypothetical examples. Avoid signals, guarantees, and personalized financial advice; include a clear disclaimer.
13. **Story sequence** — Write a five-frame story sequence that moves from a relatable problem to a useful tip to a low-pressure CTA for [offer]. Add poll, question, or slider options and avoid invented testimonials.
14. **Audience research** — Turn these audience notes into five content pillars, 10 questions to answer, and a list of phrases the audience may use. Separate observed facts from hypotheses: [notes].
15. **Retention edit** — Review this script for clarity and retention. Suggest cuts, pattern breaks, visual changes, and a stronger first five seconds without adding unsupported claims: [script].
16. **Brand voice** — Rewrite this caption in three versions: warm expert, concise practical, and playful but respectful. Keep the facts, price, and CTA unchanged: [caption and brand rules].
17. **Comment engine** — Write 15 thoughtful replies to these comments that answer the question, invite useful dialogue, and avoid sounding automated. Flag comments that need a private or professional response: [comments].
18. **Collaboration brief** — Draft a collaboration brief for [creator or business] with shared audience, concept, deliverables, responsibilities, disclosure reminder, and success signals that do not rely only on views.
19. **Analytics review** — Analyze this content log. Identify patterns in retention, saves, shares, comments, and conversions; state limits caused by small or missing data; then propose three experiments: [log].
20. **Weekly engine** — Given my niche, offers, available time, and existing assets, create a repeatable weekly workflow from idea to publish to review. Include a 30-minute minimum version and a checklist: [details].
