## 15-second clip structure
0–2s: Put the hook as spoken words and large on-screen text.
2–5s: Show the problem, proof, transformation, or chart immediately.
5–11s: Deliver 2–3 fast value beats with captions and a visual change.
11–13s: Give the payoff or reveal; remove filler.
13–15s: One CTA: save, comment, follow, book, listen, or read the full caption.
Editing checklist: vertical 9:16, clear audio, captions, one idea, cut pauses, disclose trading risk where relevant.
